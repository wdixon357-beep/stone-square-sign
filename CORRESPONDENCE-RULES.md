# Lodge demit inquiry replies

When a receiving Lodge writes to ask about a Brother who has requested a demit, prepare a reply to that specific inquiry on Stone Square Lodge No. 22 letterhead. The reply should identify the Brother and receiving Lodge and answer the standing question directly as of a stated date. Check the current dues ledger, membership record, and whether any Masonic complaints or charges are pending before stating good standing. A payment alone does not settle those other checks. If the records do not support a clear answer, hold the letter for correction rather than asserting it.

The reply is correspondence about the Brother's status. It does not itself issue a demit or say that the transfer is complete. Do not add private dues details, unrelated internal discussions, or personal circumstances when the receiving Lodge asked only about standing. Use the original verified correspondence thread for delivery when available.

The Worshipful Master may prepare the draft. Either William McDuffie, Secretary, or Adrian Reese, Assistant Secretary, may review and sign it. The first signature completes the shared request; the signed PDF uses that officer's own signature, name, and title. Sending the signed PDF remains a separate Secretary action.
